vLLM chunked prefill: what actually mattered

After two weeks of tuning a single-card deployment, three settings mattered and the rest was noise.

  1. max_num_batched_tokens = 4096. Lower starves decode, higher stalls admission under KV pressure. 4096 was the knee on our card.
  2. KV cache dtype fp8. 1.7x the pool, quality inside eval noise.
  3. A KV gate on the client side. Long-lived background jobs wait when one-minute median KV use is over 60%, so interactive turns never queue behind a batch job's prefill.
