Speculative decoding is a memory-bandwidth story

Thesis. Speculative decoding helps exactly when decode is memory-bandwidth bound and hurts when it is compute bound, so its win on a single consumer card is large and its win on a saturated server is small.

Evidence. On one 24 GB card a 7B target with a 160M drafter went from 38 to 91 tokens/s at batch 1; at batch 32 the same pair was 4% slower than the target alone, because the verification step was already compute bound.
