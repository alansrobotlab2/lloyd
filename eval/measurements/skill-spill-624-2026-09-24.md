# #624 — spilling five SKILL.md bodies into sibling files (2026-09-24)

The vault half of #624 (clauses 3, 4 and 6's nonzero delta). The code half —
`skill_lint` SIZE bucket, `spill_delta()`, per-route `skill.embedded` booking —
landed in `384f71aa`.

## What was done

Each of the five `SPILL_SAMPLE` skills was cut to ≤100 body lines. The body keeps
the procedure, every hard rule, and a `## Files in this skill` index: one line per
sibling, naming it by absolute path (`~/obsidian/skills/<skill>/<file>.md`, which
`Read` expands and `vault_read` strips) and saying when to read it. Detail moved
verbatim into siblings with the house front matter (`segment: skills`,
`type: skill`, as `powerpoint/editing.md` already had).

Rules applied:

- **Every upper-case hard-constraint line** (`prefetch._HARD_CONSTRAINT_RE`) stays
  in the body. Beyond that, the prose guardrails the live tests pin — the uptake
  gate and retrieval gate of `nightly-reflection-knowledge-write`, its prose
  ban-list, its pattern-file archive step, the knowledge-log helper, the two
  MANDATORY verification guardrails; the `:5173` scheme / TLS-trust / vault-sync
  wording rules and the round-trip refusal of `system-health-check`; the
  never-apply-SUFFIX_SAFE, degraded-graph and never-restart rules of
  `entity-resolution-sweep` — stayed in the body too. Where a body would not fit
  otherwise, hard-wrapped prose was unwrapped (same text, fewer lines); nothing
  was paraphrased out.
- Every removed `##`/`###` heading is verbatim in a sibling.
- Index lines sit well inside the 6000-char chat cut (first sibling mention at
  838–2033 chars).

## Not measured, and why

The human clause — run the five through `nightly-behavior-test` across a real
nightly cycle and compare pass rate and turns — needs live nightly runs. A
spilled skill costs a `Read` when its detail is needed; that is the risk to
watch (turns per run, and whether runs read the sibling the index names).
